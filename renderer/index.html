MEDICAL GUIDE
INJURIES
Not every hit is equally bad — wounds range from a light graze to a severe hit, and only the worst ones knock you out on impact. Lesser wounds leave you down but still conscious and able to fight/call for help; you only pass out later if blood loss actually drags you under 25%.

Low Velocity — light bleed, ~1% blood every 1.5s.
Medium Velocity — moderate bleed, ~1% blood every 1s.
High Velocity — severe bleed, ~1% blood every 0.5s. Severe enough to drop you unconscious immediately.
Fragmentation — can accompany any bleed. Doesn't change the bleed itself, just takes longer to bandage (15s instead of 5s).
Fracture — can also accompany a wound. Splint it to fix.

A hit to the head is always fatal. Multiple zones can be hit at once.
TREATMENTS
🩹 Bandage — Packs a wound. Worse wounds need more than one application before the bleeding actually stops — keep bandaging until it does. 5s per try, 15s if fragmentation is present.
🟨 Tourniquet — Limbs only, never head/torso. Stops bleeding instantly (3s to apply). It doesn't numb the limb right away — past ~4 minutes it goes numb and becomes unusable (see INCAPACITATION below, you'll get a callout when it happens), and past ~6 minutes you lose the limb entirely.
🦴 Splint — Fixes a fracture. 6s.
⚡ Stimulant — Safe wake-up on an unconscious patient. On a conscious patient it's an overdose: heart rate spikes to 200-250 and each dose taken while already spiked adds +2% risk of cardiac death (stacking). 3s.
💧 Depressant — Normally just lowers heart rate. If the patient is currently overdosing on stims, it clears the overdose outright instead. 3s.
🩸 Blood Bag — Restores 40% blood volume. 10s. Medic item.
INCAPACITATION — WHAT YOU CAN'T DO
• Unconscious or deceased — can't act, can't speak on radio
• One arm broken, or a tourniqueted arm gone numb — rifle unusable, pistol only
• Both arms broken/numb — no weapon usable at all
• Either leg broken, or a tourniqueted leg gone numb — can't walk
A tourniquet doesn't cost you the limb's use the instant it goes on — it takes ~4 minutes of cut circulation for it to go numb (you'll get an audio callout when it does). Your MY STATUS panel (and a medic's view of you) shows a live "CANNOT" summary whenever any of these apply.
